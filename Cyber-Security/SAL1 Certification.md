## TryHackMe SAL1 Certification

On 18/09/2026 I completed the Security Analyst (SAL1) certification provided by TryHackMe following the completion of their SOC Level 1 pathway.

This involved:
- 1 hour 80 question multiple choice

- 2 hour SOC Simulator #1

- 2 hour SOC Simulator #2

The multiple choice had a mix of questions around SOC fundamentals and Cyber Security in general. 
During the 2 SOC simulations, I had to triage and investigate alerts and was scored on my classification, escalation and investigative reporting.

The SOC Level 1 pathway taught me a lot of the foundational knowledge that will allow me to confidentially step into a junior analyst role. After completing each room, I utilised AI to summarize the learning material which can be found [HERE](https://github.com/AdrianGanter/Portfolio/tree/eb14682eee75c1f79e5c35af0ccc90b431be9bb4/Cyber-Security/TryHackMe/SOC%20Level%201).
This has allowed me to easily conduct revision before the exam, and allows me to continue my revision as I begin my Cyber career.

Additionally, what was vital to solidifying my knowledge, were the [practical scenarios](https://github.com/AdrianGanter/Portfolio/tree/eb14682eee75c1f79e5c35af0ccc90b431be9bb4/Cyber-Security/Labs%20%26%20Practicals) provided in the pathway. This allowed me to get hands on experience in the investigation process, and were a lot less "handholding".

With my previous work in operational security, working in a SOC environment with critical mining infrastructure and processes, and having experience working under pressure in a 12 hour day/night roster, I think I will be capable in pivoting my skills into the world of cyber security, and I am looking forward to it!
